Radar is useful when clouds hide the surface

Radar provides observations through clouds and in darkness.

Interpreting the signal is harder: water, roughness, vegetation and geometry can produce competing explanations.

SARPulse connects visual exploration with inspectable scientific evidence.

Reference checks expose the limits

A separate Biesbosch case compares NISAR with Sentinel-2 optical classifications.

ReferenceSentinel-2C L2A Scene Classification, July 15, 2026
NISAR acquisitionJuly 19, 2026 · optical reference is 4.34 days earlier
Water agreement
InterpretationSubstantial false positives remain. This is cross-sensor agreement, not field accuracy.

Cloud/invalid optical classes are excluded. Tides, actual change and classifier errors affect the comparison. Spatially correlated pixels do not provide independent validation samples.

ESA Sentinel-2 processing/SCL: https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-processing · Full reference provenance, confusion matrix and caveats are included in the app.
